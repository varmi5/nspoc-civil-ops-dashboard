{{ explainer("What do \"conjunction\" and \"fragmentation\" mean?", "

A conjunction is the technical term for two tracked objects passing close together in orbit — this is what a collision risk is called before (hopefully) nothing happens. MSH tracks these as conjunction-events. A fragmentation is a breakup incident — an object splits into new trackable debris — tracked as fragmentation-events.

Most conjunctions are routine wide misses with no computed probability at all — the general list below is bucketed by NSpOC's own \"interest\" rating (Low/Medium/High) rather than a number. The \"Requires Analysis\" table further down uses a dedicated MSH endpoint that only surfaces events past a probability threshold, with a real computed collision probability for each.
